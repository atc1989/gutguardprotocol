The 8 Markers

What your BioScan reads

Eight blood markers. One Lifestyle Inflammation Score.

CRP
C-Reactive Protein
The primary direct marker of systemic inflammation. The clearest signal that your body is in a state of chronic stress.
Normal: <1.0 mg/L
WBC
White Blood Cells
Elevated WBC with no active infection signals chronic low-grade inflammation — your immune system is quietly overactivated.
Normal: 4.5–11.0 ×10⁹/L
NEUT
Neutrophils
High neutrophils with high CRP confirms an active inflammatory burden. A key immune imbalance signal.
Normal: 50–70%
LYMPH
Lymphocytes
Low lymphocytes alongside high neutrophils — a hallmark of chronic gut-driven inflammation.
Normal: 20–40%
GLU
Fasting Glucose
Elevated fasting glucose is both a cause and consequence of gut inflammation. Your microbiome directly regulates insulin sensitivity.
Normal: 70–99 mg/dL
TRIG
Triglycerides
High triglycerides signal metabolic inflammation — one of the most commonly elevated markers in Filipino patients.
Normal: <150 mg/dL
HDL
HDL Cholesterol
Low HDL is strongly linked to gut dysbiosis. GutGuard consistently raises HDL as the microbiome restores.
Normal: >40 (M) / >50 (F)
ALT
Alanine Aminotransferase
Elevated ALT indicates gut-liver axis inflammation — a direct result of gut permeability allowing toxins to reach the liver.
Normal: 7–56 U/L

How to take it correctly

  • Take with a full glass of water, 30 minutes before or after a meal
  • Morning dose: before breakfast for maximum Urolithin-A absorption
  • Evening dose: 1 hour before sleep for L-Tryptophan's sleep effect
  • Do not crush or split capsules — nano-encapsulation requires intact delivery
  • If you miss a dose, resume normally — do not double-dose
📈

What to expect in the first week

Some patients experience mild digestive changes during days 1–4 as the microbiome shifts. This is normal.
  • Slightly looser stools (Day 1–4) — resolves by Day 5
  • Noticeably better sleep often begins Day 3–5
  • Energy improvement typically felt Day 7 onwards
If symptoms persist beyond 7 days, message Dr. Shane through your portal.
📦

Storage & handling

  • Store below 30°C, away from direct sunlight and moisture
  • Do not refrigerate unless instructed by Dr. Shane
  • Shelf life: 24 months from manufacture date (printed on pack)
  • Keep out of reach of children

What lab results does BioScan accept?

BioScan reads: CBC with differential (WBC, neutrophils, lymphocytes), Lipid panel (triglycerides, HDL), Fasting blood glucose, CRP or hs-CRP, and ALT. You don't need all 8 — BioScan scores from whatever is available.

Labs within the last 90 days produce the most accurate GLIS. Older labs can still be submitted — Dr. Shane will indicate whether a fresh panel is needed before prescribing.

Who should NOT take GutGuard

Consult your physician before starting if you are:
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Undergoing chemotherapy or radiation
  • Taking immunosuppressant medications
  • Diagnosed with active IBD (Crohn's, ulcerative colitis)
  • Under 18 years old
💊

Known interactions

  • Blood thinners (warfarin, aspirin): Inform your physician — Urolithin-A may have mild anticoagulant properties
  • SSRIs / antidepressants: L-Tryptophan requires caution — consult your psychiatrist before Grow or Power Protocol
  • Statins: No known interaction
  • Metformin: No known interaction
📋

Reporting adverse reactions

Report through your patient portal (Dr. Shane is notified instantly), via Telegram to the GutGuard support channel, or by calling the support line. Serious reactions should also be reported to the Philippine FDA ADRM at fda.gov.ph.
